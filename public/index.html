Four count-in beats precede the chart. Press D F J K as notes cross the red line. Hold long notes until their tails. Touch the lane keys on a phone. Use headphones at a comfortable volume.

Precise: ±45 ms. Close: ±140 ms. Hold release grace: 60 ms. Missing a hold loses its head and tail. Chords require each lane. Repeated keydown events are ignored. Tab away to pause; holds still require release handling. This is a local timing study, with no online ranking.
